How an engagement runs

Start on your molecule. Stop whenever the question is answered.

There is no long onboarding. The platform already holds the data, so the first session is on your product, not on a sample.

01

Send one molecule or one product. We walk through what the platform already knows about it, lens by lens, on the live system.

02

Pilot on one question set

03

Ongoing access or a one-off dossier

Keep the lenses and the Ask console open for your team, or take a single dossier on one molecule and stop there. Both are priced separately.

What comes back

A question goes in. An evidence pack comes out.

Each service follows the same path. The question is resolved to a molecule key, the right lenses read the prebuilt marts, and every figure comes back with the register it was counted from and the year it belongs to.

service · question to evidence packlive

Evidence, not advice

We show what the data says. We do not tell you what to do.

Signals, not recommendations

A dossier or a lens page shows the signal and the register it came from. It does not say launch, exit or invest. That call belongs to your team, who know what the data cannot.

Source and vintage on every figure

Each number names the register it was counted from and the year it belongs to. When two registers overlap, the page shows the larger one rather than adding them.

Counted, never estimated

Nothing is modelled, extrapolated or filled in. If a source does not hold a figure, the page says so instead of guessing.

Questions we are asked

Before you request a demo.

Does this replace analysts?

No. It gives them the counted evidence in minutes rather than weeks, with the source attached. Interpretation, context and the recommendation stay with your analysts.

Where does the data come from?

Authorised sources only: registers, records, spending and claims, and shipment data. Every figure names its source. We do not buy panels, survey or estimate.

How current is it?

Each dataset carries its own vintage, and the vintage is shown on every figure. The Data Atlas lists all 177 datasets with the period they cover, so you always know what year a number belongs to.

Can we ask our own questions?

Yes, through Ask. Type a question in plain English. The service writes and checks the query, runs it read-only, and verifies every number against the rows before it answers. How Ask verifies itself.

Do you cover India trade?

Yes. Shipment-level export and import records from India, by product, company and destination, plus global bilateral flows by product and period. See Trade Data.
