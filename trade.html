The mechanism

From shipment records to explorer views

Records arrive with names written many ways. Each is resolved to one product, one company and one country before anything is counted, and the shipment itself is kept, so any total can be opened back into the records behind it.

Global module

Beyond shipments: bilateral flows by product and period.

The shipment records show the consignment. The global module shows the corridor: which country reports sending what to whom, month by month, from January 2024 to March 2026.

  • 105 reporter countries
  • 8.4mn records
  • Jan 2024 to Mar 2026
  • bilateral by product
  • Reporter and partner. Pick either side and see the other, by product code and period.
  • Same product reference. The global module and the shipment records share one product list, so a corridor and a shipment can be read together.
  • Kept separate. Bilateral flows are refreshed on their own cycle and never blended with shipment totals.

How it is built

Counted from the records, not estimated.

Every figure on a profile or in a flow is a sum over shipments you can open. The work is in the resolution, so the counts hold when you compare one company with another.

Request a demo Trade & BD service →
  • Harmonised names. Company, country and product names are resolved through alias tables, and the resolution progress is tracked rather than assumed.
  • Shipment grain kept. Nothing is pre-aggregated away. Every total on screen opens back into the records it came from.
  • Monthly refresh. New periods are loaded as they are released; every view defaults to the latest period available.
  • Spreadsheet export. Any filtered table, profile or flow can be taken out as a spreadsheet.
  • Accounts, plans and usage credits. Profile views and exports are metered per account, so a team knows what it has used.
  • Read-only. The explorer reads the records; nothing writes back to them.
