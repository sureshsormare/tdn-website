The mechanism

One molecule spine. Every number traceable.

Sources arrive on different grains: a shipment, a prescription, an approval, a plant. Each is resolved to one canonical molecule key before anything is counted, and overlapping registers are taken at their maximum rather than summed. That is what stops the same prescription being counted twice.

Questions we are asked

Before you request a demo.

Does this replace analysts?

No. It replaces the assembly work around them. Analysts still decide what the numbers mean; they stop spending the week finding and reconciling them.

Where does the data come from?

Authorised sources only: registers, records and shipment data. Every figure names its source and vintage. Nothing is modelled or estimated.

Can we buy the data without the platform?

Yes. Each family on the Data Atlas is available as a package: harmonised files, a molecule crosswalk, a vintage manifest and an integrity report, for use in your own tools.

Is anything sent outside?

The text of a question goes to the model provider so it can draft SQL. Your data, the records and the answers stay in the deployment.

Can you build this for us?

Yes. The data layer, the lenses and the query service are built and operated by us today. We build the same stack for healthcare and pharma companies, under their brand, and hand it over.

What does a demo look like?

You name a molecule. We open the platform on it, lens by lens, then ask one question of yours in the console. No slides.

See it on your molecule first.

Send one molecule or one product. We will walk you through what the platform already knows about it, on the live system.
